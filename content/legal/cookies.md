Cookies are small files stored in your browser. thaus.co uses as few as possible.

## What we use

| Type | Name | Purpose | Duration | Needs your consent? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | `thaus_consent` | Remembers your cookie choice | 12 months | No |
| Essential (browser storage) | Chat session data | Keeps your chat conversation while the tab is open | Until you close the tab | No |
| Analytics | `_ga`, `_ga_*` (Google Analytics) | Counts visits and shows how the site is used, so we can improve it | Up to 2 years | Yes |

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for marketing.

## Your choice

When you first visit thaus.co, you can **accept** or **reject** analytics cookies. Google Analytics only loads if you accept. You can change your choice at any time with the **"Cookie settings"** link in the footer.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect parts of the site, such as the chat.

More about how we handle personal data: [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
