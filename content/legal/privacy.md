## 2. What we collect and why

| Where | Data | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |

| Contact form | Name, email, company (optional), what you need, your message | To answer your inquiry and prepare a quote | Steps at your request before a contract; legitimate interest |

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

**Please do not share sensitive information** (for example health or financial details) through the forms or the chat.

## 3. The AI chat assistant

The chat on thaus.co is an AI assistant, not a person. Your messages are processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate replies. Conversations are stored in our internal database so we can review real questions and improve our answers. If you share your contact details and ask to be contacted, they are saved as an inquiry and a person from Thaus replies. AI answers may contain mistakes and are not a binding offer.

## 4. Service providers we use

We share personal data only with providers that help us run thaus.co, and only as much as they need:

- **Vercel** — website hosting
- **Hostinger** — hosting of our automation server (our self-hosted automation software runs there; no third-party automation service receives your data)
- **Notion** — internal database for inquiries, subscribers, chat logs and orders
- **Google** — email delivery (Gmail) and website analytics (Google Analytics, only with your consent)
- **Anthropic** — AI processing for the chat assistant and newsletter writing
- **Upstash** — short-term rate limiting to prevent abuse
- **Stripe** — payment processing

Some of these providers are located in the United States or process data there. Where the GDPR applies, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as offered by each provider.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Inquiries and leads:** up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we start working together.
- **Newsletter:** until you unsubscribe. After unsubscribing we keep a record that you unsubscribed so we don't email you again.
- **Chat logs:** up to 12 months.
- **Abuse-protection data (IP, request counts):** up to 24 hours.
- **Purchase records:** as long as required by tax and accounting law (typically up to 7 years).
- **Analytics data:** according to the retention set in Google Analytics (up to 14 months).

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access your data; correct it; have it deleted; restrict or object to processing; receive it in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already happened).

To use any of these rights, email [hello@thaus.co](mailto:hello@thaus.co). We will respond within 30 days.

**EU/EEA and UK:** you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**California residents:** we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may request access to or deletion of your personal information as described above.

## 7. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed for the site to work, plus analytics cookies **if you accept them**. You can change your choice at any time. Details: see our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## 8. Clients of Thaus

When we build and run systems for our clients (for example booking or newsletter systems), we process their customers' data **on behalf of the client**, under a separate data processing agreement. In that case the client is the controller, and their own privacy policy applies. This policy covers visitors and customers of thaus.co.
